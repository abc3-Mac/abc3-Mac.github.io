Lector v1.2

Your documents, read aloud — by your iPhone, on your iPhone. Lector is Latin for reader: the one who reads aloud so others can attend to their work. PDFs, EPUBs, Word documents, and web articles become speech on the device — no account, no subscription, nothing sent anywhere. New in 1.2: see a PDF as printed, with the sentence being read highlighted on the page itself, and bring in books from your own ebook server.

Book & Series

Bad Boll: Missouri at the Crossroads

Every bridge exacts a toll. In 1948 The Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod sent its theologians to Bad Boll to meet the churchmen of a defeated Germany. The Synod was flush with relief funds and a century out of its isolation. Seven summers of conferences followed. They gave Missouri the International Lutheran Council. They gave it the argument over Scripture that ended in Seminex. And they cost it Hermann Sasse, the one German who saw what was coming. He was not invited. Albert B. Collver III tells the story from the unpublished files of Concordia Historical Institute, as a historian who has served the churches on both sides of the bridge. This is the enterprise Missouri forgot, and the price it is still paying.

📚 Forthcoming from Concordia Historical Institute 📖 Manuscript complete · 374 pages
🇩🇪 Seven summers of conferences, 1948–1954, from London to Gothenburg
🗂️ Built from the unpublished Behnken, Bretscher and Graebner files at CHI
✉️ Five Hermann Sasse letters, translated and printed in full
🛣️ Three roads out: the ILC, Seminex, and the Synodical Conference
🎼 Franzmann’s Thy Strong Word and its Bad Boll papers
🖋️ Foreword by Lawrence R. Rast Jr.; Geleitwort by Werner Klän
